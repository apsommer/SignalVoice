HOW IT WORKS

SignalVoice monitors market conditions and identifies structured signal events based on price behavior. When a signal is triggered, it is delivered instantly to your device and can be spoken aloud through voice alerts.

The app is designed to integrate into your existing workflow, giving you awareness without requiring constant screen time.
